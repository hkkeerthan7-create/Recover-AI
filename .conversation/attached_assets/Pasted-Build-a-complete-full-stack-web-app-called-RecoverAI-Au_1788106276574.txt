RECOVERY LOGIC:
Use deterministic rule-based AI-style scoring.

Failure reasons:
- Insufficient funds
- Network timeout
- Authentication failure
- Card expired
- Bank decline
- Daily limit exceeded

Typical logic:
- Temporary failures such as network timeout and insufficient funds = higher recovery potential.
- Authentication failure = medium potential.
- Expired cards = low potential.
- Daily limit = retry after cooldown.
- Bank decline = medium/low potential.

Score:
80–100 = High
60–79 = Medium
0–59 = Low

Actions:
- RETRY_NOW
- RETRY_AFTER_COOLDOWN
- REQUEST_AUTHENTICATION
- SEND_PAYMENT_REMINDER
- ESCALATE_MANUAL_REVIEW
- SKIP

For each decision provide a human-readable reason.
