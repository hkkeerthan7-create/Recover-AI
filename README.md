## RecoverAI

## AI-Powered Revenue Recovery Decision Engine

RecoverAI is an AI-assisted revenue recovery platform designed to help businesses analyze failed payments and determine the most appropriate recovery action.

Instead of treating every failed payment the same way, RecoverAI analyzes payment failure signals, generates a recovery score, applies business safety policies, and recommends an appropriate action such as retry, manual review, or skip.

> **Note:** RecoverAI uses a synthetic environment for demonstration purposes. No real payments are processed.

---

##  Project Overview

Failed payments can result in significant revenue loss for businesses.

Automatically retrying every failed payment can lead to:

- Unnecessary payment retries
- Poor customer experience
- Repeated failures
- Increased operational workload
- Risky automated decisions

RecoverAI introduces an intelligent decision layer between payment failures and recovery actions.

### How it works

Failed Payment
      ↓
Failure Classification
      ↓
AI Recovery Scoring
      ↓
Policy & Guardrail Check
      ↓
Recovery Decision
      ↓
Audit Log
